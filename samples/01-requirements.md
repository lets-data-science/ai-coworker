Requirements from the analytics lead

We send a weekly sales report to six regional managers every Monday by 9:00. Today one analyst builds it by hand in a spreadsheet, which takes about three hours.

What we need:
- The same numbers every week, without copy and paste.
- Managers can see their own region, but not other regions' customer names.
- Someone other than the current analyst can fix it if it breaks.
- Total running cost under 150 a month.

Nice to have: a chart of the last 12 weeks. Not needed: real-time data.
